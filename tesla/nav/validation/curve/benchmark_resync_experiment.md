# Offline navigation - benchmark report

Generated by `python3 -m tesla.nav.evaluate --all --seeds 30` at commit `d30f32f`, navigation-parameter hash `d2fea20a6ae3`, 4410 seeded runs.

**Read this first.** Conditions are split into *clear* (wrong road >= 3 sigma from the route), *marginal* (2-3 sigma) and *ambiguous* (< 2 sigma), judged from the nominal GPS sigma, not from how the navigator performed. They are reported separately and **never combined into one success percentage**: a navigator cannot be expected to tell apart roads that are within one GPS error of each other, and averaging those in would hide how it behaves where it can. Every proportion has a Wilson 95% interval; with 30 seeds an observed 0/30 still allows a true rate up to ~11%.

Reporting `ambiguous` instead of guessing is **not** counted as a wrong road. Persistent ambiguity (an episode longer than 100 m, or still open at the end of the drive) is reported in its own column so that it cannot flatter the other numbers.

Definitions are in the module docstring of `tesla/nav/evaluate.py`. Overrides applied: none (defaults).
